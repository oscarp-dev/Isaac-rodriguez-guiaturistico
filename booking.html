Walking Tour · Alicante, Spain

Alicante:
Where Muslim
& Christian History Meet

Duration 2h 30min
Language English
Groups Small
Start Auditorio la Concha

Discover the history and hidden stories of Alicante beyond what any guidebook will ever show you. Starting at the iconic Explanada de España, we walk through the most authentic corners of the city — the streets that whisper centuries of history, and the places only a local knows.

In just 2 hours and 30 minutes, you'll experience over 1,000 years of Muslim and Christian history, the city's vibrant gastronomy, and the soul of a neighbourhood that time forgot. All of this with a local guide who lives Alicante every single day.

  • Professional guide — Isaac Rodriguez
  • 9 stops across the city
  • Muslim & Christian history narration
